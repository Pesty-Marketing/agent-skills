# Pyramid Principle source notes

Source: [Alexander Lyon on Pyramid Principle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jtx01xQNw5A). These are Pesty-authored notes and adaptations of the teaching. Consult the original video for its full examples and wording.

The pyramid organizes a message into an overall conclusion, supporting points, and the evidence for each point. Lead with the answer. Group reasons at a comparable level of detail and keep evidence under the reason it supports.

The vertical check asks whether evidence supports its parent argument and whether the arguments support the conclusion. The horizontal check asks whether sibling arguments are distinct and cover the audience's question. MECE is a useful grouping heuristic. A coherent structure does not establish causation or prove that a claim is true.

Lyon uses three supporting points in examples. The skill lets the substance determine the count. Source examples illustrate organization rather than independently verified business findings.
