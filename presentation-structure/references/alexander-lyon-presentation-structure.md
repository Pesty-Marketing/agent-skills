# Presentation Structure source notes

Source: [Alexander Lyon on Presentation Structure](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FPhJ8LEJrU). These are Pesty-authored notes and adaptations of the teaching. Consult the original video for its full examples and wording.

The opening uses Situation, Complication, Question, and Resolution. The question arises from the complication; the resolution answers it. That same resolution becomes the body's central message. Develop supporting arguments and evidence without introducing a second thesis.

Lyon demonstrates the structure with a hypothetical proposal for using AI on a team. Its business claims are illustrative, not independently verified findings.

His closing advice is to signal the ending, repeat the central message, and recap supporting points when useful. He says he does not know of a single prescribed McKinsey closing framework. The skill therefore treats the closing as his advice and does not require a stock closing phrase.
