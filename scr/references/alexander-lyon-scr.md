# SCR source notes

Source: [Alexander Lyon on SCR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TBGF33plj8Q). These are Pesty-authored notes and adaptations of the teaching. Consult the original video for its full examples and wording.

SCR connects the background an audience needs, the complication that blocks a goal, and the proposed action that addresses it. It can guide short written messages, spoken recommendations, or a presentation opening. Keep the background brief and place the new development in the complication. Adapt length to the medium.

The source illustrates the structure with workplace collaboration, recruiting, and restaurant competition. Those scenarios demonstrate message structure; their business claims are not evidence for a current client decision.
