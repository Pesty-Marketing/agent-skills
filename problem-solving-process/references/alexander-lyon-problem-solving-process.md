# Problem Solving Process source notes

Source: [Alexander Lyon on Problem Solving Process](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qOp3vv0JIZA). These are Pesty-authored notes and adaptations of the teaching. Consult the original video for its full examples and wording.

Lyon explains six stages: define the problem, dissect it, prioritize, develop a work plan, analyze, and synthesize recommendations. He describes this as one version rather than a definitive McKinsey standard.

His observed practice sessions generally stopped after work planning. The skill therefore defaults to framing and an investigation plan, while allowing analysis and synthesis when requested. Investigation priorities are questions to test; they are not established causes or proven solutions. The source's 80/20 language is a prioritization heuristic, not a quantified outcome guarantee.

The linked McKinsey podcast is titled [How to master the seven-step problem-solving process](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-to-master-the-seven-step-problem-solving-process). Keep that title distinct from Lyon's six-stage explanation. Qualify recommendations when the evidence is limited.
